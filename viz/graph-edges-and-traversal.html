An edge is one line. An entry is one item in a .neighbors list. Every edge is written down twice — once from each end — so 6 edges become 12 entries.
Your loop walks entries, not edges. That's why the total is 4 node visits + 12 entry walks = 16, and never 4 × 6 = 24.
V and E move independently. Same 4 nodes in a straight line would have only 3 edges and 6 entries — so the node count alone can't tell you the cost. Both terms are needed: O(V + E).
